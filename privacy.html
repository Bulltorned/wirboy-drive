Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026.

What the app accesses

Only files and spreadsheets that a team member explicitly shares with the assistant's Google account, mainly one shared working folder. The app signs in only as that assistant account.

How the data is used

To read working files the team provides and to save the assistant's results into the shared folder. Nothing else.

Storage

Working copies are kept on a private server run for the team, with access limited to the people who operate it. Copies are deleted when the team stops using the tool, or earlier on request.

Sharing

We do not sell the data, share it for advertising, or use it to train models. The assistant runs on Anthropic's Claude, which processes content only to answer the team's requests, under Anthropic's terms.

Removing access

Unshare the folder in Google Drive, or remove the app under Google Account, Security, Your connections to third-party apps and services.

Contact

Use the support email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
